TrustBank Case Brief

Riya Sharma, Credit Manager at TrustBank, must evaluate a ₹8,00,000 loan request from Arjun Sharma. Historical records show:

  • P(Default)=5%
  • P(Missed Payment | Default)=80%
  • P(Missed Payment | No Default)=10%

The applicant has a missed-payment history. Should the loan be approved?
