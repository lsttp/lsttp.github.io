Business Context

Managers often make decisions under uncertainty. A single observation may be misleading. Repeated observations reveal stable patterns. This simulator demonstrates how evidence accumulates and supports better decision making.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand sample space
  • Understand randomness
  • Understand probability distributions
  • Observe the Law of Large Numbers
  • Develop evidence-based thinking
